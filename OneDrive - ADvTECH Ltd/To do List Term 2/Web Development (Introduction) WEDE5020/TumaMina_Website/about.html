About TumaMina

TumaMina is a trust and verification service for online marketplaces. The name comes from isiZulu — "Tuma" means send, "Mina" means me. Send Me.

Our Mission

To make buying and selling second-hand goods in South Africa safe, trustworthy, and accessible for everyone.

Our Vision

To become the go-to verification platform for all online marketplaces in South Africa, eliminating scams and building a community of trusted buyers and sellers.

The Problem We Solve

Every day, South Africans lose money to online marketplace scams. You find something you want, send the money, and then nothing shows up. Or worse — a box of bricks arrives. TumaMina puts a real, trusted person between you and the seller.

How We Started

TumaMina started as an idea while scrolling through Facebook Marketplace. Seeing people get scammed over and over, we realised there needed to be a trusted middleman — a "friend in every city" who can go look at something for you before you pay.
